
## Setting & Society
* **Era:** 12,000 years ago (the agricultural period)
* **Location:** An archipelago named **`Lasupika`**. (The name has no literal meaning)
* **People:** The **`Lansupikan`** people (The name has no literal meaning)
* **Economy:** Capitalists who rely on farming and trade. They are economically advanced for their time, using un-named currency (**`he`**), tariffs, and limited ships (**`wapa`**)

---

## Cultural Concepts & Philosophy
* **Safety:** Safe (**`lunsal`**) is defined as *"not whole"* (**`lun`** + **`sal`**)
* **Dreams & Illusions:** 
  * Mockery (**`besere`**) is based on the sound of blowing one's nose (*bzrrr*)
  * An illusion (**`besui`**) is a sly form of mockery
  * A dream (**`besuini`**) is a *positive illusion*, while a nightmare (**`besuinu`**) is a *negative illusion*
  * Goodnight (**`lunsal besuini`**) translates to *"safe dream."*
* **Morning Greeting:** Unknown for the current period, in future shall focuses on the person being greeted (reconnecting after sleep/illusions) rather than the day or weather
* **Friendship & Starfish:** 
  * Starfish have no food value and are only seen as gifts, so the word for starfish and gift is the same (**`sili`**, derived from **`sil`**, to give)
  * Friend (**`pesili`**) literally means *"I would share a starfish (gift) with you."*

## The Language: *Lasu uwu*

### Phonology & Sound Rules
* **Consonants:** `p`, `b`, `t`, `k`, `s`, `h`, `m`, `n`, `w`, `l`, `y` (/j/), `r` (/ɹ/)
* **Vowels:** `a`, `e`, `i`, `o`, `u`, and the diphthong `ui` (/ui/)
* **Syllable Structure:** Standard syllables are open (CV) or closed with nasal (CVn/Vn). **CVC is permitted at the end of words** (eg, `sal`, `lun`, `ker`)
* **Pronunciation Note:** `-er` is pronounced strictly as `\ɚ\`
* **Historical Sound Shift:** Ancient `f` shifted into `p` (eg, proto *feker* $\to$ `peker`, *fe-* $\to$ `pe-`, *fesili* $\to$ `pesili`)

#### Grammar
* **Word Order:** **SOV** (Subject – Object – Verb)
* **Verbs:** No verb conjugations at this current period
* **Pronoun System:**
  * **Singular Pronouns** are emotive (they have 6 emotives: **Neutral**, **Positive**, **Negative**, **Assertive**, **Humble**, **Sly**)
  * **Plural Pronouns** are strictly emotionless/neutral:
    * `namu` = We (Inclusive)
    * `ensu` = We (Exclusive)
    * `tasu` = You (Plural)
    * `wanu` = They (People)
    * `eku` = They (Objects/Goods)

### Established Lexicon

* **`na` / `ta` / `wa` / `ya` / `ele`**: Singular pronouns (I, You, He, She, It) with their respective emotive variants.
* **`lun`**: Not (prefix)
* **`sal`**: Whole (adjective)
* **`lunsal`**: Safe (adjective)
* **`besere`**: Mock (verb)
* **`besui`**: Illusion (noun, sly)
* **`besuini`**: Dream (noun, positive)
* **`besuinu`**: Nightmare (noun, negative)
* **`lunsal besuini`**: Goodnight (phrase)
* **`ker`**: Cut (verb)
* **`pe-`**: Share (prefix)
* **`peker`**: Share (verb)
* **`sil`**: Give (verb)
* **`sili`**: Gift / Starfish (noun)
* **`pesili`**: Friend (noun)
* **`he`**: Money / Currency (noun)
* **`pela`**: Trade / Exchange (verb)
* **`wapa`**: Ship / Boat (noun)
* **`wuku`**: Sail (verb)
