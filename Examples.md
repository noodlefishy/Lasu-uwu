`pela` = trade
`wa` = ship

> [!QUOTE] Na wa pela.
> "I am trading a ship"
(**Neutral**: it is just a fact)

> [!QUOTE] Ran wa pela.
> "I AM TRADING A SHIP!"
(**Assertive**: This is my final offer, take it or leave it)

> [!QUOTE] Unu wa pela.
> "I have to trade a ship away..."
> (**Negative**: I am desperate for money and i am forced to sell my ship)

> [!QUOTE] Sin wa pela.
> I offer to trade my ship to you
> (**Humble**: Trying to please a port authority)

> [!QUOTE] yo wa pela.
>  I am trading a ship :3
>  (**Sly**: I am trading a ship but there is a leak)
