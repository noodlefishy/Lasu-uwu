
safe = not whole

`lun`(not) + `sal`(whole)

`lunsal` = safe

---
dream = illusion

illusion = to play with/ to trick / **to mock**
~~mock = bad playing~~
mock is derived from the sound of blowing somebody's nose
`besere` (the onomatopoeia for bzrrr) = mock

---

a sweet mocky is a dream
**joyful** (-ni) and **negative** (-nu) 

illusions are **sly** (-uin)
`besui` = illusion 
from a sly form of mockery (`besere` + `-ui`)

---

`besuini` = dream 
a dream is a joyful illusion (`besui` + `-ni`)

`besuinu` = nightmare
a nightmare is a negative illusion (`besui` + `-nu`)

---

goodnight = safe dream

`lunsal besuini` = goodnight
